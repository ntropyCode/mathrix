How to read the field

Each named region is a working register. Bits start at the right and wrap upward as the value grows. Cyan cells are 1; dark cells are 0. A magenta outline marks carry information or the active position. Hover a cell for its bit position.

Negative values use an explicit sign with binary magnitude. Fractions retain separate numerator and denominator registers. This is the chosen algorithm’s state, not a trace of your CPU.

Use + − * / ^, parentheses, decimal numbers, 0b101, or 0xff. Plain 10 means decimal ten. log(x) is the natural logarithm; log(x, base) chooses a base. Powers require integer exponents.

Fractions and integer operations are exact. Logarithms are numerical approximations, not certified error bounds. Runs retain the most recent 512 states; restart to replay earlier states.
