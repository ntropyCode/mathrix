# Thoughts

Exploratory notes retained from the discussion on 2026-09-18. They preserve
questions and arguments without promoting hypotheses to established results or
changing [research intent](../intents/research.md).

The direction developed in the discussion is:

> Investigate computation through the differences a question requires, the
> relationships that make them accessible, and what remains recoverable across
> the chosen system boundary.

## Starting points

- [Atomic observers, space, time, and complexity](atomic-observers-space-time-and-complexity.md)
  introduces the proposed common basis of spatial and temporal descriptions.
- [Spatial relations as temporal reconstruction](spatial-relations-as-temporal-reconstruction.md)
  examines the uncounted capabilities an observer supplies to a visualization.
- [Why observers distinguish space and time](why-observers-distinguish-space-and-time.md)
  proposes a benefit in prediction and action, without deriving subjective
  experience or claiming an established evolutionary history.

## Ten retained insights

1. Configurations and histories can share a description.
2. Being determined is different from being accessible.
3. Problem and solution are roles relative to a question.

These are developed in
[configurations, histories, and access](configurations-histories-and-access.md).

4. An answer may omit the causal input information needed for recovery.
5. Learning can establish correlation without erasing information.
6. Relationships can carry information invisible to isolated observers.
7. Determinism fixes consequences; reversibility requires recoverable origins.

These are developed in
[answers, recoverability, and entropy](answers-recoverability-and-entropy.md).

8. Reversible rotation can participate in a computation whose answer does not
   recover its input history: [when rotation computes](when-rotation-computes.md).
9. Restoring a configuration and preserving a new record of its restoration
   impose different requirements:
   [restoration and its records](restoration-and-its-records.md).
10. Descriptions, implementations, and different kinds of limits need separate
    accounting:
    [descriptions, implementations, and formal limits](descriptions-implementations-and-formal-limits.md).

## Later notes

- [What a third dimension means](what-a-third-dimension-means.md) separates
  intrinsic axes from layout choices and records a 3D prototype's dependency
  checks.

Literal physical interchangeability of space and time, universal irreversibility,
and an unavoidable infinite regress of reconstruction remain unestablished.
None of these notes resolves P versus NP or derives physical laws from difference
alone.
